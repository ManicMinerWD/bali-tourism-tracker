Data sources & method

    How to update this tracker:
    1. Open data.js in this repo.
    2. Add the new month's figures to the monthly array (and bpsGate if you have gate data).
    3. Update lastUpdated at the top of data.js.
    4. Commit + push — the site rebuilds automatically on GitHub Pages.
    Monthly international/domestic figures come from BHA (international + domestic together) and BPS Bali (foreign arrivals by gate). Annual totals come from BPS. Country breakdowns: full-year 2025 from BPS/BHA/Antara; March 2026 snapshot from BHA.
