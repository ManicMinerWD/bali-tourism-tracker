          Short-term rental / Airbnb market private platforms

          STR data comes from private platforms (Airbnb, AirDNA) — not government statistics. BPS only publishes hotel TPK. The island-wide STR occupancy is roughly 57–65% depending on source and methodology.

            Data sources & method

              How to update this tracker:
              1. Open data.js in this repo.
              2. Add the new month's figures to the relevant array (arrMonthly, gate2026, tpkStar, tpkNonStar, losMonthly2026, etc.).
              3. Update lastUpdated at the top of data.js.
              4. Commit + push — the site rebuilds automatically on GitHub Pages.
              Monthly international/domestic figures: BHA (international + domestic) + BPS Bali (foreign arrivals by gate). Annual totals: BPS. Country breakdowns: full-year 2025 from BPS/BHA/Antara; March 2026 snapshot from BHA. Hotel occupancy (TPK) and LOS: BPS Bali monthly press releases. GDP: BPS Bali quarterly. STR market: AirDNA / Airbtics / Villa Finder / Hospitable (private — not official statistics). Property prices & ADR: Reid Real Info 2025 Market Report via Investlandbali.com. Limitations: BPS TPK is for star-rated hotels only — it does not measure villa/STR occupancy. No public monthly source gives zone-level STR occupancy or average nightly price. GDP is quarterly and lagged. STR data is from private platforms with their own methodology. All figures should be cross-checked before investment decisions.
